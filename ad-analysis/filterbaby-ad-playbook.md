## 2. The 6 winning video formats (beat-by-beat)

### Format A — Founder story / mini-doc (their #1 video ad, 140s)
Analyzed: media-0002. Highest-impression video in the account.
1. **Hook (0–3s):** personal pattern-interrupt — "I didn't get into Harvard, but…" + face to camera
2. Credibility: "3rd-gen healthcare practitioner" lab-coat shot
3. Personal pain: skin rash close-ups, mirror shots ("this was MY skin")
4. Category exposé: competitor products on shelves, Amazon listings, "full of loopholes"
5. Third-party proof: app screen-recordings scoring competitor filters badly
6. Origin: "so we built our own" → factory/manufacturing footage
7. Product reveal: hero shots of the unit, slow rotate
8. Claims stack: contaminants removed, efficacy-over-time line graph
9. Retail + association badges, before/after customer photos
10. Guarantee CTA

### Format B — Offer montage / BOGO (ranks #4, #6 — 30–40s)
Analyzed: media-0004, media-0006.
1. **Hook:** bold text overlay "We're Not Doing This Again 😮‍💨" over product macro
2. Rapid UGC cuts (1.5–2.5s each): unboxing → install → face wash → shower → before/after
3. Offer stated twice, visually (two units in hand = "two filters")
4. Emotional beat: "give one to someone whose skin you care about"
5. Scarcity CTA: "stock is already low / grab yours before it's gone"

### Format C — Comparison test (46s, creator whitelisting)
Analyzed: media-0009.
1. **Hook:** "POV you finally replace your skincare filter for one that actually works"
2. The villain: screenshot of a cheap Amazon filter, trash-can shot
3. Live test: digital chlorine meter in two glasses; tap vs filtered flasks (yellow vs clear)
4. Mechanism: "starts at 99%, stays 95% for 3 months"
5. Clinical stat overlay + review screenshot with before/after
6. Soft CTA

### Format D — Humor / relationship POV (20s)
Analyzed: media-0011.

2. Product unbox → him checking his hair → install montage
3. Checklist overlay: Chlorine ✅ Chloramine ✅ Microplastics ✅

### Format E — In-store "Buy or Bye" review (38s)
Analyzed: media-0032.

### Format F — Dirty-cartridge testimonial (37s, male UGC)
Analyzed: media-0008.

## 3. Hook library (translated concepts, not lines)

- Personal confession + credibility ("I'm a [credential], and my skin was still a disaster")
- Visual disgust (dirty cartridge / limescale to camera, zero context)
- Comment-reply bubble (answers the #1 objection as the hook)
- POV relationship humor
- "We tested the leading filters" authority
- Offer-first text overlay ("We're not doing this again" + deal)
- Stat shock ("X million homes still get water through lead pipes")

## 4. Shared structural DNA (apply to every Bluvana ad)

- First 2 seconds decide everything: face or product + bold overlay text, always
- 1.5–3s cuts, captions on 100% of spoken word (large, centered, white with stroke)
- One proof prop per ad (dirty cartridge, meter test, before/after) — never all of them
- Checklist overlays with green checkmarks for contaminant claims
- Offer stated visually (two units in frame) not just verbally
- CTA = urgency + risk reversal ("before it's gone" + "30-day guarantee")

## 5. ⚠️ Claims Bluvana CANNOT borrow

Filterbaby's authority angles rest on real substantiation. A dropshipped filter has none of these:
- "Clinically tested / doctor-developed", wound-healing or skin-cell-growth percentages
- "American Hair Loss Association approved", "IAPMO / NSF-177", "9X% effective after N liters"
- Any specific disease/skin-condition claims (eczema before/afters are also a Meta policy risk)

**Bluvana-safe claim set:** "reduces chlorine", "reduces limescale buildup", "filters sediment and
rust", "softer-feeling water", "easy 2-minute install", "replaceable cartridge", visual proof via
dirty-vs-clean cartridge and limescale comparisons (real photos of the real product — these are
honest and already planned in the PDP shot list). If the supplier has ANY test reports, get them
and cite only what's in them.

## 6. Seedance production matrix

| Shot type | Seedance text-to-video? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Person talking to camera (hooks, UGC) | ⚠️ Risky | AI people read as AI in ES markets too; consider real creator clips or avatar tools for face shots |
| Product macro / hero rotates | ✅ Only as image-to-video | Feed real Bluvana product photos as first frame — never let it invent the product |
| Shower/bathroom ambience, steam, water | ✅ Yes | Text-to-video works well, no product in frame |
| Install close-ups (hands + real product) | ⚠️ image-to-video only | Anchor with real photos; hands deform in text-to-video |
| Dirty vs clean cartridge | ❌ Shoot real | This is the honesty proof — must be the actual product |
| Screen recordings, graphs, badges, overlays | ❌ No | Do in CapCut/edit, never generate |
| Before/after skin | ❌ No | Policy risk + dishonest; skip entirely |

**Recommended pipeline per video:** real product photos (from PDP shoot) → Seedance image-to-video
for product/ambience clips → edit + captions + overlays in CapCut → ES voiceover (ElevenLabs or
recorded) → text overlays in Spanish.

## 7. Spanish adaptation notes

- Hook-first scripts must be re-angled, not translated. "POV" culture exists in ES TikTok but
  "POV:" is often kept in English; voiceover should be neutral LATAM Spanish unless targeting Spain.
- Water-quality fear angle works but needs local stats (hard water in Spain/CDMX/Guadalajara,
  chlorine smell in tap water is a widely recognized complaint — lead with sensory "smells like a
  pool" instead of EPA stats).
- Gifting angle (BOGO) maps well to Día de las Madres / Navidad / reyes — plan offer calendar.
