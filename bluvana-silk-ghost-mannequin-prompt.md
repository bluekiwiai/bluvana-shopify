# Bluvana Silk — category shelf image prompts

Ghost mannequin cut-outs for the six homepage category tiles.
Upload a photo of the real garment, then paste the block below with one
GARMENT line swapped in. Generate all six with the same block so the row
looks shot in one session.

Settings: 1024x1024, PNG, transparent background.
API: set `input_fidelity: "high"` to preserve details from the uploaded photo.

---

## Master prompt

Ghost mannequin product photograph of the garment in the uploaded photo.

WHAT TO MAKE
The garment worn on an invisible body. It holds a natural three dimensional
human shape with filled shoulders, chest and sleeves, but there is no person,
no mannequin, no hanger and no stand. The neckline is hollow and open so the
inside back of the collar is visible through the opening. Soft natural creases
where fabric would fold on a body. The fabric hangs under its own weight.

FIDELITY, THIS MATTERS MOST
Reproduce the uploaded garment exactly. Keep its exact color, sheen, piping,
contrast trim, buttons, button placement, pockets, collar shape, cuffs, hem,
drawstring and stitching. Do not restyle it, do not add or remove details, do
not add a pattern, do not change the proportions, do not invent a logo or care
label. This is a real product being photographed, not a new design.

CAMERA, IDENTICAL ON EVERY SHOT
Straight on, eye level with the chest, 85mm lens look, no tilt, no angle.
Garment centered horizontally. The bottom hem sits about 10 percent above the
bottom edge of the frame. Do not zoom to fill the frame. Keep the camera
distance fixed so a long robe naturally reads taller than a short set.

LIGHT, IDENTICAL ON EVERY SHOT
One large soft key light from the upper left at roughly 45 degrees, gentle fill
from the right. The satin sheen runs as a soft highlight down the folds and the
right side falls into soft shadow. Realistic specular roll on silk. No hard
flash, no rim light, no colored light.

BACKGROUND
Fully transparent. No backdrop, no floor, no ground shadow, no reflection.
Clean edges suitable for cutting out.

DO NOT INCLUDE
People, faces, hands, skin, mannequin parts, hangers, clips, stands, hang tags,
text, watermarks, props, furniture, plants, or any second garment unless the
garment line below asks for one.

GARMENT: [swap this line]

## The six garment lines

1. Women's Pajamas
   A women's silk camisole and shorts set, camisole on top with the shorts
   positioned below it as one composition.

2. Men's Pajamas
   A men's silk pajama shirt with a notched collar and matching pants, shirt on
   top with the pants below it as one composition.

3. Robes
   A full length silk robe, belt tied in a soft knot at the waist, hanging to
   full length so the skirt of the robe falls open slightly at the hem.

4. Short Sets
   A short sleeve silk top and matching shorts, top on top with the shorts
   below it as one composition.

5. Couples Sets
   Two silk pajama sets side by side, one in each colorway, the sleeve of the
   left set overlapping the right set slightly so they read as a pair.

6. Gifting
   A closed rigid gift box, ribbon tied off center, with one edge of silk
   escaping from under the lid. No garment on a body form for this one.

## QC before you keep one

- Pull the hex off the garment and compare it to the real product. Models drift
  color on saturated reds, and rouge has to be right.
- Check buttons and piping. That is where invention shows up first.
- Lay all six side by side at the same size. If one is noticeably bigger or lit
  from a different side, regenerate it.
- Generate two or three per garment and pick.

## Where they go

Theme editor > Silk home > Category shelf > "Category N cut out" image pickers,
in this order: Women's Pajamas, Men's Pajamas, Robes, Short Sets, Couples Sets,
Gifting. Transparent PNGs sit directly on the paper background; white
rectangles will punch holes in it.
