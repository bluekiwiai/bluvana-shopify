Keep it
Bluvana.

If it looks clinical, generic or staged, change it.

Do

  • Show the product solving a space problem
  • Use daylight or direct flash
  • Show tile, wood, stone, water and fabric
  • Cast different ages, bodies and personal styles
  • Keep one brand color in the room
  • Keep grain, shadow and reflection

Avoid

  • Generic white marble spa images
  • Props that hide the product
  • Blue splashes and science graphics
  • Plastic skin or surfaces
  • Only showing large bathrooms
  • Color edits that change the product finish
