# Bluvana Google Merchant Center readiness audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-20  
**Live store:** `https://trybluvana.com` (Shopify store `em3i5y-qa`)  
**Scope:** Public storefront, authenticated theme preview, Shopify Admin, checkout stopped before payment, and accessible Merchant Center  
**Rubric:** GMC Audit v2026.08.11 — 219 checks  
**Final state:** **BLOCKED — pre-submission readiness audit**

**Coverage:** 219/219 checks classified — **47 Pass · 64 Fail · 74 N/A · 34 Unable to verify**

Google makes the final approval and enforcement decision. This audit did not request a review, submit a payment, place an order, change store/account settings, or connect the unrelated Merchant Center account.

## Executive conclusion

Bluvana is not ready to connect or submit to Google Merchant Center. The live storefront is password-protected, normal product URLs redirect to `/password`, and `sitemap.xml` returns 404. Shopify Payments is not fully set up and no alternative provider is configured. There is also no Google & YouTube sales channel, no Bluvana Merchant Center account/source, and no Bluvana products in Merchant Center.

The only accessible Merchant Center account is **Astrion (ID 5448150487)** for **shopastrion.com**. It is incomplete (3 of 6 onboarding tasks, missing business address and website verification, zero products) and is not Bluvana. It must not be connected to this store.

Even after the access/payment/account blockers are fixed, several account-wide risks remain: placeholder legal text and return address, a published `555` phone number not present in Shopify, false unconditional “Free US shipping” claims, contradictory return terms, four dead footer links, variant deep links that ignore the requested variant, absent Product/Offer schema, unsubstantiated health/performance claims, contradictory imported-review counts, and incomplete variant/category data.

## Actionable findings

### Critical — storefront is inaccessible to Google and ordinary shoppers

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; account-wide.
- **Evidence:** Signed-out requests to the homepage, product, collection, contact, cart, and policy paths redirect to `https://trybluvana.com/password`; the authenticated preview bar says `Password protected`; `https://trybluvana.com/sitemap.xml` returns 404.
- **Why it matters:** Google cannot crawl landing pages or verify prices, availability, policies, or checkout.
- **Fix:** Remove Online Store password protection, then recheck every key URL signed out and confirm the sitemap returns XML.
- **Recheck:** `curl -IL https://trybluvana.com/products/shower-filter` must end on the product URL (not `/password`), and `/sitemap.xml` must return 200 XML.

### Critical — no functional payment provider

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; account-wide.
- **Evidence:** Shopify Admin → Settings → Payments shows Shopify Payments `Complete setup`; no additional payment provider is configured. Checkout renders a payment form, but the account configuration is incomplete.
- **Why it matters:** A real customer may be unable to complete payment; this blocks launch/review readiness.
- **Fix:** Complete the legitimate Shopify Payments setup or configure an approved provider, then place a merchant-authorized controlled test order.
- **Recheck:** Provider status must be active (not test-only) and a controlled payment/refund test must succeed.

### Critical — no Bluvana Merchant Center product source

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; account-wide.
- **Evidence:** Installed channels are Online Store, Point of Sale, and Shop/Agentic; Google & YouTube is absent. The only accessible GMC account is unrelated Astrion/shopastrion.com, incomplete, and contains zero products.
- **Why it matters:** There is no correct account, claimed Bluvana domain, source, product processing, shipping/returns configuration, or diagnostics to review.
- **Fix:** Create/select the legitimate Bluvana Merchant Center account, verify/claim `trybluvana.com`, complete business identity, install/connect Google & YouTube, and sync the intended catalog. Do not use Astrion.
- **Recheck:** Shopify and GMC must show the same Bluvana account ID/domain, a current source, and at least one processed in-stock product.

### High — variant URLs produce price and availability mismatches

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; all six Shower Filter Pro variants and likely systemic custom-template behavior.
- **Evidence:** Live requests using `?variant=43201614774334` (Chrome / Filter only, $79.95), `?variant=43201614807102` (bundle, $99.95), and sold-out Gold `?variant=43209304670270` all render the same default $99.95 bundle and Chrome copy. Canonical remains the generic product URL.
- **Why it matters:** Shopify/Google normally links variant offers with variant parameters. Google can see a different price, image, or availability than the submitted offer.
- **Fix:** Make the custom product template read the URL variant on initial load, select the exact Shopify variant, and render its price, availability, image, SKU, and purchase state before Google/user interaction.
- **Recheck:** Test every one of the 12 variant URLs signed out; the requested offer must be selected and match cart/checkout.

### High — legal and identity information is unfinished or inconsistent

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; site-wide/policies/admin.
- **Evidence:** Terms contains `[LINK]`, a merchant note, `[agent name]`, `[INSERT TRADING NAME]`, `[INSERT BUSINESS ADDRESS]`, `[INSERT BUSINESS PHONE NUMBER]`, registration and VAT placeholders. Refund policy contains `[INSERT RETURN ADDRESS]`. Site publishes `(800) 555-0199`; Shopify store contact says `No phone number`. Admin entity is `My Store - entity`; website does not publish a verifiable legal entity/address; Contact information policy is not set.
- **Why it matters:** This is strong misrepresentation/business-transparency risk and prevents Google from reconciling the merchant identity.
- **Fix:** Publish the accurate legal/trade name, genuine support details, and operational return address; remove every template note/placeholder; align Shopify and GMC fields. Do not invent an address or phone.
- **Recheck:** Search all live HTML/policies for `[`, `INSERT`, `NOTE TO MERCHANT`, `agent name`, and `555`; reconcile all identity fields.

### High — shipping promise contradicts checkout

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; all product pages, especially offers under $70.
- **Evidence:** Every product page says `Free US shipping`. Shopify charges $8 Standard below $70, free Standard at $70+, and $15 Express. A $49.95 cartridge order reached shipping with $8 due; a $99.95 bundle received free Standard.
- **Why it matters:** The landing-page shipping claim is false for the $19.95/$49.95 cartridges, $59.95 Multi Spray, and $69.95 Luxe Flow offers.
- **Fix:** Change claims to `Free US shipping on $70+` everywhere or change checkout rates to honor unconditional free shipping.
- **Recheck:** Test representative carts below, at, and above $70.

### High — return promise contradicts the refund policy

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; catalog-wide sale/bundle offers.
- **Evidence:** Product pages promise `30-day risk-free returns` and `money back`; collection cards label products `Sale`; refund policy says sale items cannot be returned and still contains a placeholder return address.
- **Why it matters:** Customers cannot determine whether advertised offers are returnable.
- **Fix:** Choose one truthful rule, remove the contradiction, state who pays return shipping, and publish the real return method/address.
- **Recheck:** Compare product, collection, refund policy, cart, checkout, and GMC return policy word-for-word.

### High — unsupported health/performance claims and review provenance risk

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed claims; high confidence contradiction; provenance partially unverified.
- **Evidence:** All product templates state or repeat claims including `Stops the post-shower itch from the very first shower`, `Calmer skin in 30 days`, heavy metals are absorbed through skin, chlorine fumes are breathed in, acne is drastically reduced, and brown water becomes clear. The same pages show both `4.9 from 250+ verified buyers` and `4.9 from 1,000+ verified buyers`, while saying reviews were collected for the exact filter/showerhead. Project artifacts contain Temu sourcing/review data, but the live page does not clearly name the source.
- **Why it matters:** Extraordinary health/water-quality claims require reliable substantiation; contradictory or misattributed review counts can support a misrepresentation finding.
- **Fix:** Retain only claims supported by product-specific, legally usable evidence; qualify filtration limits; disclose review source/import status accurately; show one consistent count.
- **Recheck:** Build a claim-to-evidence file and independently reconcile the rendered review database/counts.

### High — broken navigation and missing product schema

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; four footer links and all four products.
- **Evidence:** `/pages/faq`, `/pages/track-order`, `/pages/accessibility`, and `/pages/do-not-sell` render 404 pages. All four product pages expose only Organization JSON-LD; no Product/Offer data exists.
- **Why it matters:** Dead support/legal links reduce transparency; missing Offer markup removes a primary crawler consistency signal and automatic-update path.
- **Fix:** Create or remove dead links (link the actual Shopify opt-out page) and emit valid variant-aware Product/Offer JSON-LD.
- **Recheck:** Link-check the full footer and validate every product/variant in a structured-data tester.

### High — catalog fields are not submission-ready

- **Confidence / scope:** Confirmed; all 12 variants unless noted.
- **Evidence:** All 12 barcodes are blank; all 12 weights are `0.0 lb`; 10 variants do not track inventory; the two Gold variants are tracked/sold out but have blank SKUs; all country-of-origin and HS fields are blank; the Gold bundle is misspelled `cartirdges` and has the same $79.95 price as Gold filter-only. Shower Filter Pro is uncategorized and other category suggestions were not established as accepted. The two Chrome/Black filter bundles have Shopify compare-at `$99.80` while price is `$99.95`; the live page instead shows `$139.80`.
- **Why it matters:** Identifiers, inventory, shipping weight, category, variant labels, and reference prices can be wrong or absent when the channel is installed.
- **Fix:** Verify real GTIN/MPN/brand facts rather than fabricating them; populate valid SKUs, shippable weights, category, inventory policy, and truthful compare-at values; correct option text/Gold price.
- **Recheck:** Export all variants after the source is corrected and compare Shopify → channel → processed GMC attributes.

## Material verification limits

- No Bluvana GMC account/source exists, so live product diagnostics, processed attributes, account shipping/returns, marketing methods, review/cooldown state, and approval counts are not applicable yet.
- The contact form was inspected but not submitted because an audit does not authorize sending an external message.
- Mobile behavior could not be reliably emulated in the available signed-in preview; responsive metadata exists, but `SITE-007` remains unable to verify.
- Legal entity, genuine phone/email operability, product manufacturer/GTIN/MPN, supplier authorization, fulfillment origin/timing, safety substantiation, tax nexus, and whether imagery/text was AI-generated remain partly or wholly unverified.
- Checkout stopped before payment as required. A successful transaction requires separate authorization after a real provider is active.
