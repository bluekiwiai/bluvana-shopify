# Pose-Transfer Prompts — Purple Satin Reference Set

These prompts are designed for use with:
1. one of the six uploaded images as the **pose reference**, and
2. a separate **target image** whose model, outfit, scene, lighting, and visual identity must remain unchanged.

For every prompt below, use the reference image for **pose and body language only**.

---

## Pose 1 — Front Three-Quarter Stance with One Arm Extended Sideways

Use the uploaded reference image **only as a guide for the model's stance, torso angle, arm placement, head direction, gaze, and overall fashion posture**.

Preserve everything else from the target image: keep the target model's identity, facial features, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, body proportions, clothing, accessories, footwear, environment, furniture, background, lighting, camera perspective, lens characteristics, and overall visual style unchanged.

Reposition the target model into a pose closely matching the reference:

- Stand upright in a relaxed three-quarter fashion stance.
- Keep the torso mostly facing the camera, rotated only slightly to one side.
- Shift the body weight subtly onto one leg so the hips are naturally asymmetrical.
- Keep the shoulders lowered and relaxed.
- Extend one arm outward to the side at approximately waist-to-lower-chest height.
- Keep that extended arm mostly straight but not locked.
- Let the wrist remain soft and neutral, with the hand relaxed and fingers naturally separated.
- Allow the opposite arm to hang down alongside the body with a gentle bend at the elbow.
- Keep the lower hand relaxed near the outer thigh.
- Turn the head slightly away from the camera toward the same general side as the extended arm.
- Direct the eyes off-camera rather than straight into the lens.
- Keep the chin neutral to slightly elevated.
- Use a calm, composed, fashion-editorial expression.
- Keep the chest open and the torso elongated without arching the back excessively.

Match the reference's **upright three-quarter stance, one arm extended sideways, opposite arm relaxed down, subtle hip shift, sideward head turn, and off-camera gaze** as closely as possible.

**STRICT POSE TRANSFER ONLY.** Do not copy the reference model's face, hairstyle, skin tone, purple satin garments, robe, lace, jewelry, room, sofa, wall, lighting, color palette, or photographic treatment.

If the target outfit does not contain a robe, cape, jacket, or flowing outer layer, **do not add one**. Reproduce the skeletal pose without inventing fabric movement.

Avoid stiff elbows, claw-like fingers, exaggerated hip thrusting, deformed hands, changed clothing, or artificial fabric flaring.

## Pose 2 — Dynamic Three-Quarter Back Pose with Bent Front Leg

Use the uploaded reference image **only as a guide for body orientation, leg geometry, arm placement, shoulder rotation, head position, and overall movement**.

Keep the target model's identity, face, hair, body proportions, skin tone, clothing, accessories, background, lighting, and visual style unchanged.

Reposition the target model into this pose:

- Rotate the body away from the camera into a strong three-quarter back orientation.
- Keep the hips turned mostly away while the upper torso twists slightly toward the camera side.
- Shift most of the weight onto the rear/supporting leg.
- Bring the opposite leg forward and outward.
- Bend that knee clearly, creating a strong diagonal line through the thigh.
- Keep the forward foot planted or lightly weighted in a physically natural position.
- Bend the nearer arm at the elbow and let the forearm angle downward in front of the torso.
- Keep that wrist relaxed and the hand hanging naturally with soft fingers.
- Extend the opposite arm slightly behind or outward from the body.
- Keep the second elbow gently bent.
- Turn the head back toward the camera side, showing the face in side profile or soft three-quarter profile.
- Keep the chin approximately level.
- Direct the gaze forward, off-camera, or slightly toward the camera depending on what is natural for the target face.
- Preserve a fluid, mid-turn feeling without making the body look unstable.
- Keep the spine curved only naturally; do not exaggerate the arch.

Match the reference's **three-quarter back orientation, forward bent leg, asymmetric arms, soft wrist position, torso twist, and profile head turn** as closely as possible.

**Do not transfer the reference model's appearance or styling.** Do not copy the purple robe, lingerie, lace details, hairstyle, skin tone, jewelry, room, sofa, wall, or lighting.

If the target has no flowing outer garment, do not create one. The sense of movement should come from the **body position**, not invented clothing.

Avoid impossible hip rotation, dislocated shoulders, twisted wrists, warped knees, floating feet, or altered target clothing.

Reposition the target model into a pose closely matching the reference:

- Extend the legs outward in a relaxed manner.
- Keep the knees naturally aligned and not rigidly locked.

- Place the fingertips lightly near the temple, cheekbone, or jawline.
- Keep the hand relaxed, with no pressure against the face.

- Keep the shoulders relaxed.
- Hold the head upright with a slight tilt toward the raised hand.
- Direct the eyes toward the camera.
- Use a calm, softly confident expression.
- Maintain a relaxed, elegant lounging posture without slouching excessively.

Match the reference's **diagonal seated recline, extended legs, one hand near the cheek/temple, opposite hand resting across the lap, slight torso lean, and direct gaze**.

**STRICT POSE TRANSFER ONLY.** Do not copy the reference model's face, hairstyle, purple satin clothing, lace, jewelry, mustard sofa, wall, lighting, or color treatment.

## Universal Pose-Lock Reinforcement

Append this paragraph to any prompt if the generator begins copying the reference model, clothing, or environment:

> **STRICT POSE TRANSFER ONLY. Treat the reference image solely as a skeletal/body-language guide. Transfer only joint positions, limb angles, weight distribution, torso orientation, hand placement, head angle, gaze direction, seated/standing geometry, and overall stance. The TARGET IMAGE is the sole source of identity, face, facial structure, hair, skin tone, body appearance, clothing, accessories, footwear, furniture, environment, background, lighting, camera perspective, texture, and color. Do not import, recreate, blend, or imitate any robe, lingerie, lace, hairstyle, facial traits, furniture, scenery, lighting, or color palette from the pose reference.**

## Optional Anatomy Reinforcement

Append this if the generator struggles with hands, arms, legs, or seated anatomy:

> Keep human anatomy natural and physically plausible: exactly two arms, two hands, five fingers per hand, two legs, and two feet. Preserve believable shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, and ankle articulation. Hands must connect correctly to wrists; feet must connect correctly to ankles. No duplicated limbs, merged fingers, floating hands, twisted joints, warped knees, impossible leg crossings, or asymmetrical limb lengths.
