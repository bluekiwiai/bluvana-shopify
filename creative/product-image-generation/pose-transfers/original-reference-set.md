# Pose-Transfer Prompts — Original Reference Set (6 Images)

Use these prompts when you have:
1. a **pose reference image** (one of the six images), and
2. a **target image** containing the model whose identity, outfit, and scene you want to keep.

The reference image should control **pose and body language only**.

---

## Pose 1 — Looking Down, One Hand Holding the Hem

Use the uploaded pose-reference image **only as a guide for pose, posture, body positioning, hand placement, head angle, expression direction, and framing**.

Keep the person in the target image completely unchanged in identity, facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, skin tone, outfit, accessories, scene, background, lighting, camera style, and overall aesthetic.

Reposition the target model into a pose that closely matches the reference:

- Stand in a relaxed three-quarter fashion pose, with the torso mostly facing the camera but angled slightly.
- Shift the hips subtly to one side so the stance feels natural and asymmetrical rather than rigid.
- Keep the legs comfortably separated, with one leg extending slightly outward or forward to create a gentle diagonal line.
- Let one arm hang naturally downward beside the body, with the hand resting lightly near the upper thigh.
- Bend the opposite elbow and bring that hand across the lower torso.
- With that hand, lightly pinch or hold a small section of the existing garment near the lower hem or waist area, without changing the garment's design.
- Keep the fingers soft, naturally curved, and anatomically correct.
- Lower the chin and angle the head slightly downward and toward one side.
- Direct the eyes downward rather than toward the camera.
- Use a soft, relaxed smile or pleasantly calm expression.
- Keep the shoulders relaxed and slightly uneven in a natural way.
- Preserve a graceful editorial/fashion-catalog feeling rather than an exaggerated runway pose.

Match the reference's **hip shift, arm asymmetry, hand placement, downward gaze, head tilt, and relaxed body language** as closely as possible while adapting naturally to the target person's anatomy and clothing.

**Do not transfer anything else from the reference.** Do not copy the reference person's face, hair, makeup, skin tone, jewelry, clothing, fabric, room, wall, cabinetry, lighting, color palette, or photographic style.

Avoid stiff elbows, clenched hands, twisted wrists, extra fingers, distorted limbs, extreme hip popping, dramatic back arching, or changing the target garment merely to accommodate the pose.

## Pose 2 — Three-Quarter Back Pose, Arm Raised Overhead

Use the uploaded pose-reference image **only as a guide for pose, posture, body orientation, arm placement, head position, weight distribution, and framing**.

Keep the target model's identity, face, hair, body proportions, skin tone, clothing, accessories, scene, lighting, and visual style unchanged.

Reposition the target model into a pose closely matching the reference:

- Rotate the body away from the camera into a strong three-quarter back view.
- Keep the hips and lower body more directly presented toward the camera while the upper torso twists slightly to one side.
- Shift most of the body weight onto one leg.
- Extend the opposite leg diagonally outward to create a long, elegant line through the hip and thigh.
- Raise one arm upward and curve it over the head.
- Bend that elbow clearly so the forearm forms an arch above the head.
- Place the raised hand gently on the top/back of the head or lightly into the hair.
- Keep the raised shoulder natural rather than shrugged.
- Allow the other arm to remain relaxed and unobtrusive along the far side of the body.
- Turn the head into a soft side profile, angled slightly upward.
- Keep the eyes softly closed or nearly closed.
- Use a serene, faintly smiling expression.
- Create a gentle curve through the spine and torso, but keep it anatomically believable and not excessively arched.
- Let the pose feel confident, elegant, and editorial.

Match the reference's **three-quarter back orientation, hip placement, raised-arm arc, hand-on-hair position, side-profile head angle, and extended-leg line** as closely as possible.

**Do not transfer the reference model's appearance, hairstyle, lingerie, jewelry, background, architecture, lighting, or color palette.** Keep all of those elements from the target image.

Avoid excessive spinal twisting, impossible shoulder rotation, malformed raised arms, floating hands, fused fingers, distorted hair, exaggerated butt/hip reshaping, or changing the target clothing.

## Pose 3 — Front-Facing Relaxed Stance, Head Tilted Back

Use the uploaded pose-reference image **only for the pose, stance, head angle, body language, and composition**.

Preserve the target model's identity, face, hairstyle, body shape, outfit, accessories, background, lighting, camera perspective, and visual style.

Adjust the target model into the following pose:

- Stand mostly front-facing toward the camera.
- Keep the feet/legs moderately separated to create a stable, relaxed fashion stance.
- Shift the weight slightly onto one leg so the hips are not perfectly level.
- Let both arms fall naturally beside the body.
- Keep the elbows relaxed and very slightly bent rather than locked.
- Let both hands remain open and soft near the outer thighs.
- Keep fingers naturally separated and relaxed.
- Lengthen the torso and keep the shoulders lowered.
- Tilt the head gently back and slightly toward one side.
- Raise the chin modestly.
- Keep the eyes closed or softly lowered, matching the serene expression of the reference.
- Use a subtle, content expression with relaxed lips.
- Let the hair fall naturally according to the target image rather than copying the reference hairstyle.
- Keep the overall pose simple, elegant, and effortless.

Match the reference's **front-facing stance, slight hip asymmetry, relaxed arms, head-back angle, raised chin, and eyes-closed expression** as closely as possible.

**Do not transfer any clothing, lace details, hairstyle, jewelry, wall panels, lighting, colors, or facial traits from the reference.**

Avoid a dramatic backbend, overly raised chin, rigid arms, splayed fingers, unnatural shoulders, extreme leg spread, or direct eye contact with the camera.

Keep the target model's identity, facial features, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, outfit, accessories, environment, lighting, and camera style unchanged.

- Shift the hips slightly to one side.

- Let one arm hang naturally down beside the body, with the hand resting near the upper thigh.
- Bend the opposite elbow and bring that hand across the lower torso.
- Lightly pinch or hold a small section of the target garment near the waist or hem, without redesigning or lifting it excessively.
- Keep the wrist neutral and the fingers soft.
- Turn the head gently away from the body's angle, looking off-camera to the side.
- Keep the chin neutral to slightly raised.
- Use a warm, relaxed smile.
- Keep the shoulders down and natural.
- Preserve a polished fashion-catalog look with understated movement.

Match the reference's **three-quarter torso angle, hip shift, one-hand-at-thigh position, opposite hand lightly gathering the garment, off-camera gaze, and easy smile**.

**Do not copy the reference model's face, hair, skin tone, outfit, lingerie design, jewelry, room, background, or lighting.** Preserve those details from the target image.

Avoid dramatic garment pulling, exposing or changing parts of the target outfit, stiff hands, twisted wrists, distorted arms, exaggerated hip curvature, or changing the camera angle unnecessarily.

## Pose 5 — Full-Body, Hand on Hip, Bent Leg Crossing In Front

Use the uploaded pose-reference image **only as a guide for the full-body pose, weight distribution, leg placement, arm placement, head direction, and overall composition**.

Keep the target person's identity, face, hair, body proportions, skin tone, outfit, accessories, footwear, environment, lighting, and photographic style unchanged.

Reposition the target model into a pose closely matching the reference:

- Stand upright in a full-body fashion pose.
- Keep the torso mostly facing the camera with only a slight natural angle.
- Place most of the body weight on one straight supporting leg.
- Bend the opposite knee and bring that leg inward across the front of the supporting leg.
- Let the bent leg's foot rest lightly on the toes or ball of the foot, creating an elegant crossed-leg silhouette.
- Keep the knees and ankles anatomically natural and avoid tangling the legs.
- Place one hand at the side of the waist/hip with the elbow pointing outward.
- Let the opposite arm hang relaxed down the front/side of the body.
- Keep that relaxed hand soft with natural finger spacing.
- Turn the head gently to the side, looking off-camera.
- Keep the chin slightly lifted.
- Use a soft, confident smile or composed pleasant expression.
- Keep the shoulders relaxed and the torso elongated.
- Make the overall body language graceful and effortless.

Match the reference's **one-hand-on-hip silhouette, outward elbow, single-leg weight bearing, bent leg crossing in front, pointed/toe-resting foot, sideward head turn, and relaxed smile** as closely as possible.

**Do not copy any appearance, hair, outfit, lingerie, jewelry, room, flooring, wall design, lighting, or colors from the reference.**

Avoid impossible ankle crossings, fused feet, extra toes, warped knees, exaggerated hip shifts, a hand floating away from the waist, or changing the target outfit or footwear.

## Pose 6 — Full-Body, One Hand on Hip, Other Hand Near Hair

Use the uploaded pose-reference image **only as a reference for pose, gesture, body positioning, head angle, gaze direction, and full-body framing**.

Preserve the target model's identity, facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, skin tone, outfit, accessories, scene, lighting, and visual style exactly.

Reposition the target model into this pose:

- Stand mostly front-facing in a relaxed full-body fashion stance.
- Shift the weight primarily onto one leg.
- Bring the opposite leg slightly across the front of the supporting leg, with the knee softly bent.
- Let the crossing foot rest lightly on the toes or forefoot.
- Keep the legs close and elegant rather than widely separated.
- Place one hand on the waist/hip, with the elbow bent outward.
- Raise the opposite hand toward the hair or side of the head at about shoulder height.
- Keep the raised hand relaxed, as though lightly touching, adjusting, or brushing the hair.
- Bend that elbow naturally without lifting the shoulder.
- Lower the chin slightly and tilt the head toward the raised hand.
- Direct the eyes downward.
- Use a soft, natural smile.
- Keep the torso elongated and shoulders relaxed.
- Preserve an understated, candid fashion-catalog feeling.

Match the reference's **hand-on-hip gesture, opposite hand raised near the hair, crossed-leg stance, slight knee bend, downward head tilt, downward gaze, and gentle smile** as closely as possible.

**Do not transfer the reference person's face, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, outfit, lingerie details, jewelry, room, wall panels, lighting, floor, or color treatment.**

Avoid malformed hands, fingers merging into hair, exaggerated elbow angles, floating hands, impossible leg crossings, warped feet, excessive hip curvature, or changing the target model's clothing to match the reference.

## Universal Reinforcement Line

If the image model keeps copying the wrong things, append this to any prompt:

> **POSE TRANSFER ONLY. The reference image is not an appearance reference. Copy only skeletal pose, joint positions, weight distribution, hand placement, head angle, gaze direction, and body orientation. The target image is the sole source of identity, face, hair, body appearance, clothing, accessories, environment, lighting, camera style, and color.**
