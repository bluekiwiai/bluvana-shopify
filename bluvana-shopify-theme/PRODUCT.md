# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are people furnishing, organizing, or upgrading a small bathroom. They need products that fit limited floor, wall, counter, and storage space without making the room feel clinical or compromised. This audience and its exact demographic mix are inferred from the supplied brief and brand guide.

## Product Purpose

Bluvana is a curated ecommerce destination for bathroom design, appliances, storage, fixtures, textiles, and accessories made for smaller bathrooms. Success means helping a shopper quickly understand what will make their room work better, find the right category, and feel confident enough to browse or buy.

## Positioning

Bluvana treats small bathrooms as full living spaces rather than problems to hide. Products earn their place by saving space, reducing visual clutter, or improving a daily routine while preserving warmth and personality.

## Operating Context

The store is expected to grow to roughly 200 to 300 dropshipped products. The homepage must remain useful as the catalog expands and should organize discovery by room problem, product category, and daily routine. Current Shopify inventory is sparse, so dynamic product areas must populate gracefully as products and collections are added.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shopify Online Store 2.0 using the Horizon theme.
- Current scope is the storefront landing page, not product-page design.
- Product and collection links should use native Shopify routes and data.
- Preview and QA happen on a development theme; the live theme is not published without explicit approval.
- Customer-facing commercial claims, reviews, prices, guarantees, shipping promises, and product specifications must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Bluvana.
- Purpose line: better products for small bathrooms.
- Official supplied Bluvana wordmark and monogram are the only approved logos.
- Voice is concise, warm, useful, and benefit-led.
- Visual identity follows the supplied Bluvana design system: Ocean Blue, Deep Tile Green, Sun Gold, Mineral Ivory, Paper, and Ink; Bodoni Moda display type; Archivo body and utility type.
- Photography is hyper-realistic, warm, clean, organized, and editorial without appearing staged or synthetic. Main imagery includes people in believable daily routines. Supporting imagery may focus on rooms and objects.

## Product Principles

1. Make the space gain visible.
2. Organize a large catalog around real bathroom problems and routines.
3. Keep shopping language concise and easy to scan.
4. Show believable small rooms, people, materials, and product scale.
5. Earn trust through clarity and restraint rather than unsupported claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The storefront should meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction expectations, support keyboard navigation and reduced motion, preserve legible text at mobile sizes, and represent a varied customer base without tokenism.
